Privacy Policy

Pinterest Archive · Last updated: 2026-09-22

Pinterest Archive ("the app") is a personal creative-archive project that lets a user turn their saved images into a browsable 3D gallery ("tunnel"), with search, personal notes, and the ability to follow other users of the app. This page explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

1. Account data

When you create an account, we store your email address, a username you choose, and (if you sign up with a password) a securely hashed password. If you sign in with Google or Kakao instead, we receive the basic profile info those providers share (typically your email and display name) to create your account — we never see or store your Google/Kakao password.

2. Pinterest data

If you choose to connect your Pinterest account, the app requests read-only access to your own boards and pins, for the sole purpose of importing them into your personal archive inside the app. Specifically:

  • We only read data belonging to the Pinterest account that authorizes the connection — never any other user's boards or pins.
  • We never post, edit, or delete anything on your Pinterest account. Access is read-only.
  • Imported pin data (image, title, description, link) is copied into our own database so it can be displayed in the 3D gallery; we do not continuously sync or scrape beyond what you explicitly import.
  • You can disconnect Pinterest access at any time from your Pinterest account settings, which revokes the app's access token.

3. Content you create in the app

Personal notes, custom keywords, and follow relationships you add inside the app are stored so the app can show them back to you (and, for follows, to the people you choose to follow). This content is not shared outside the app or sold to anyone.

4. Who else sees your data

The images and metadata in your archive are visible to other logged-in users of the app if they visit your archive ("tunnel") — this is a core feature of the app, similar to a public portfolio or mood board. Your account email and password are never shown to other users.

5. Third-party services we use

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles account authentication.
  • Google / Kakao — optional sign-in providers, used only if you choose them.
  • Pinterest API — used only if you choose to connect your Pinterest account, to import your pins.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete individual pins from your archive at any time inside the app. To request full account deletion, contact us at the email below.
